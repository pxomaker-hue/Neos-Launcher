## 0.7.0

- Compact, wrapping icon navigation on narrow screens.
- Paginated filename search through the Nextcloud file index.
- Background loading of all note pages while keeping the current draft open.
- Batched retrieval of CardDAV contacts from accessible address books.
- CalDAV recurrence display, simple recurrence creation, and editing of supported series and occurrences.
- Copy the text and attachments from an IMAP draft into a new message.
