## 0.7.4

- On wide screens, Free layout lets you pin each widget to a row and column of the twelve-column grid. Drag its handle or enter coordinates with the Position button.
- The drop preview flags blocked placements. Automatically positioned widgets fill available space around pinned widgets.
- Positions are saved per account and restored when returning to Free layout on a wide screen.

## 0.7.3

- Hide home widgets from their cards and restore them through the hidden widgets drawer.
- In manual layout mode, drag the bottom corner of a card to resize it in steps.
- Preferences now retain layouts with every widget visible.

## 0.7.2

- Mail and Talk unread badges use a soft cyan halo with a subtle grid, with and without tab labels.

## 0.7.1

- Mail and Talk show unread counts in the dashboard navigation, including in compact icon mode.
- The Mail count covers inboxes using the statistics returned by Nextcloud Mail.

## 0.7.0

- Compact, wrapping icon navigation on narrow screens.
- Paginated filename search through the Nextcloud file index.
- Background loading of all note pages while keeping the current draft open.
- Batched retrieval of CardDAV contacts from accessible address books.
- CalDAV recurrence display, simple recurrence creation, and editing of supported series and occurrences.
- Copy the text and attachments from an IMAP draft into a new message.
